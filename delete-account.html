Delete your Pico Ludo account or data

Pico Ludo, by Custard

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pico Ludo and tap your profile at the top left of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away and the app starts you afresh as a guest.

Without the app

Email us from any address. Include your username (if you created an account) or your display name, and roughly when you last played.

Request deletion by email

We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can ask us to delete parts of your data without closing your account, for example your statistics (games played and won), your display name, or your saved progress.

Request data deletion by email

Tell us your username (or display name) and which data you want deleted. We confirm and complete it within 30 days.

Data on this device only: uninstalling Pico Ludo, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it straight away.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account (player ID, username, password, display name).
  • Your saved progress: coins, hints, unlocked and purchased items, equipped styles.
  • Statistics and copies stored on your device.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by Google Play, under Google's policies (needed for refunds, tax and fraud prevention). Purchased coins and items can't be restored after deletion.
  • Advertising data held by ad partners under their own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
