PICO LUDO - DIE-CUT STICKERS - PRINT NOTES

What's here
- 22 stickers (7 dice, 8 seeds, 7 boards), one PDF each.
- PicoLudo-die-cut-ALL-items.pdf has the same 22 stickers, one per page.

Cutting
- Contour cut (die-cut) each sticker along the vector path in the spot colour "CutContour".
- Cut line: 0.25 pt, 100% magenta alternate, set to overprint. It is a cut path only - do not print it.

Size and artwork
- Supplied at about 67-76 mm (2.6-3 in) per sticker. Scale up or down if you need another size.
- Artwork: RGB, 300 dpi.
- White border of 2.4 mm inside the cut line, plus 1.5 mm white bleed outside it.

Material
- Print on white vinyl (gloss or matte laminate recommended).
- Clear or holographic vinyl needs a white ink layer under the artwork. That layer is not included.
