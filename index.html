Why it's different

More ways to kick

Land on a lone seed to kick it. Side kick an enemy across the arm and take its square. Back kick behind you, and if you land behind your start, it counts as a full lap.

Your house rules

Soft for classic Ludo, Wicked for every twist, or build your own: blockades, safe squares, a turn timer and Labourer & Master. Agreed before you roll, so nobody argues halfway through.

Play anywhere

Against the computer from Easy to Impossible, 2 to 4 friends on one phone, or friends on the same Wi-Fi. No mobile data needed.

Big characters

A cartoon 3D board, tumbling dice, glowing Adinkra symbols and three announcers: a cheerful Little Girl, a Shrewd Captain and a wise Old Man.

How a turn works

  1. Roll. Every roll becomes a number card. Roll a six and you go again: 6, 6, 2.
  2. Choose. Pick a card and the seed it moves. Add cards together to reach further.
  3. Kick. Send enemy seeds home, form walls, race into your home column.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Pico Ludo is free to play and shows ads. Optional in-app purchases include coins, hints, dice and board styles, announcer voices and Remove Ads.

Do I need the internet?

No. You can play the computer or friends on one phone completely offline. Wi-Fi play connects phones on the same network and doesn't use mobile data.

Can I play with friends far away?

Not yet: multiplayer works on the same Wi-Fi network for now.

Which phones?

Android 8.0 or newer. iPhone isn't available yet.

Who is it for?

Players aged 13 and over.

How do I learn the twists?

Tap “How to play” in the game for a short guided tutorial, and use hints during play: they recommend your best move and explain why.
