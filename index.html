Why it's different

More ways to kick

Land on a lone seed to kick it. Side kick an enemy across the arm and take its square. Back kick behind you, and if you land behind your start (anywhere but your first lane), it counts as a full lap.

Your house rules

Family, Soft, Casual or Wicked, from gentle to every twist, or build your own: blockades, safe squares, a turn timer and Labourer & Master. Agreed before you roll, so nobody argues halfway through.

Play anywhere

Online with friends anywhere: create a game, share the code, play. Or against the computer from Easy to Impossible, 2 to 4 friends on one phone, or friends on the same Wi-Fi with no mobile data. Closed the app mid-game? Carry on where you left off.

Big characters

A cartoon 3D board, tumbling dice, glowing Adinkra symbols and three announcers: a cheerful Little Girl, a Shrewd Captain and a wise Old Man.

Fantasy Battle

Everyone is dealt Adinkra emblems, each with a power: freeze a rival, shield your seeds, leap a wall, teleport. And beware Ananse the spider, whose tricks can help you or hold you. Level up to unlock 28 emblems and new stages.

Talk without typing

Quick chat in online games: ready-made lines and emojis to cheer, tease and taunt. Try Akasanoma, the talking parrot with Ghanaian lines.

How a turn works

  1. Roll. Every roll becomes a number card. Roll a six and you go again: 6, 6, 2.
  2. Choose. Pick a card and the seed it moves. Add cards together to reach further.
  3. Kick. Send enemy seeds home, form walls, race into your home column.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Pico Ludo is free to play and shows ads. Optional in-app purchases include coins, hints, Adinkra emblems, Fantasy Battle stages, chat packs, dice, board and seed styles, announcer voices and Remove Ads. Online games and Fantasy Battle cost coins to enter, and you earn coins by playing.

Do I need the internet?

No. You can play the computer or friends on one phone completely offline. Wi-Fi play connects phones on the same network and doesn't use mobile data. Online games need an internet connection.

Can I play with friends far away?

Yes. Tap Play Online, create a game and share its code (on WhatsApp, for example). Your friends type the code to join, and you play together wherever you are.

Which phones?

Android 8.0 or newer. iPhone isn't available yet.

Who is it for?

Players aged 13 and over.

How do I learn the twists?

Tap “How to play” in the game for a short guided tutorial, and use hints during play: they recommend your best move and explain why.
